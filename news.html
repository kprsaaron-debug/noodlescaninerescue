4 June 2026 MILESTONE

We Are Now an Official Registered Charity

We are proud to announce that Noodle’s Canine Rescue IOM has been officially registered as a charity on the Isle of Man — Charity No. 1387. This is a major step forward in our mission to help reunite missing dogs with their families using thermal drone technology.

Recent Operation

Successful Recovery Using Thermal Drone

Our team recently located a missing dog in challenging coastal terrain using our thermal drone capabilities. The dog was safely reunited with its family within hours. This operation highlights the vital role thermal imaging plays in rapid search and rescue.

More updates coming soon. Follow us on social media for real-time operation updates.
