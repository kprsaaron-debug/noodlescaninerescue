Latest from our canine search and rescue operations and charity milestones

4 June 2026 MILESTONE

Officially Registered as Charity No. 1387

We are delighted to announce that Noodle’s Canine Rescue IOM has been officially registered as an Isle of Man charity (No. 1387). This milestone, just over a year after our formation, recognises the hard work of our volunteers and the vital service we provide to the Island’s dogs and families. We thank everyone who supported the registration process.

This status strengthens our governance, fundraising capability and ability to collaborate with emergency services.

January 2026 OPERATION

Successful Recovery of Toffee – 50+ Hour Multi-Agency Search

Our teams led a complex search for a missing dachshund in treacherous Foxdale and St Marks Country Park terrain. Working alongside landowners, police, coastguard, Castletown fire station and dozens of public volunteers, thermal drones played a crucial role in locating the dog after more than 50 hours missing. The happy reunion was a powerful reminder of the value of coordinated canine search and rescue.
