Report a Missing Dog

If your dog is missing, please report it immediately using our official form.
Our drone, ground and scent teams will respond as quickly as possible.

Official Missing Dog Report Form

This secure form is the fastest way to alert our full team. Please provide as much detail as possible — it helps us deploy the right resources immediately.

Open Report Form Now

Opens in a new tab • Mobile friendly

Prepare This Information Before Submitting

  • Your name and best contact number
  • Dog’s name, breed, age, colour & distinctive features
  • Last known location and exact time last seen
  • Recent clear photos (upload in form)
  • Any relevant medical conditions or medications
  • Whether the dog is wearing a collar / has a microchip

The more accurate the information, the faster and more effectively we can search.

What Happens After You Report?

  1. Immediate triage: Our duty team reviews the report and assesses urgency, location and resources needed (drone, ground team, scent dog).
  2. Rapid deployment: Teams are mobilised — often within the hour for high-priority cases. We coordinate with police/coastguard where appropriate.
  3. Regular updates: You will receive updates via phone/text as the search progresses.
  4. Reunification support: Once located, we assist with safe recovery and return to you.

We aim to respond to every report as quickly as humanly and safely possible. Please also post on local Facebook groups and contact local vets/groomers — community eyes help enormously.
