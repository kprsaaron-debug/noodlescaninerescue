HOW WE HELP

Canine Search & Rescue.
Powered by Technology & Teamwork.

Thermal Drone Searches

Our professional-grade thermal drones detect heat signatures in woodland, coastal terrain, plantations and at night — dramatically increasing recovery rates even in the most challenging conditions on the Isle of Man.

Ground Teams & Volunteers

Highly trained volunteers conduct systematic ground searches using proven patterns, navigation and radio protocols. They work seamlessly alongside drone teams and scent dogs across rural, coastal and urban-fringe areas.

Scent Dog & K9 Handlers

Our Dog Handling team works with trained scent dogs, including our own collie Noodle in advanced scent discrimination training. They provide precise location capability and integrate closely with drone intelligence and ground teams.
