OUR STORY

Born from a simple idea: no dog should be lost forever.

Noodle’s Canine Rescue was founded to provide a dedicated, professional canine search and rescue service for missing dogs on the Isle of Man using thermal drone technology.

HOW WE HELP

Advanced Canine Search.
Compassionate Rescue.

Thermal Drone Searches

We use high-resolution thermal drones to locate missing dogs in woodland, coastal areas, and at night.

Ground Teams & Volunteers

Trained volunteers and K9 handlers conduct coordinated canine search and rescue operations.

Rapid Response

We maintain a ready team for rapid deployment across the Isle of Man.
