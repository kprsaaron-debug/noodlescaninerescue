HOW WE HELP

Advanced Search.
Compassionate Rescue.

Thermal Drone Searches

Our DJI Matrice 4T drones with high-resolution thermal imaging allow us to detect heat signatures in woodland, coastal cliffs, and at night.

Coordinated Ground Teams

We work with trained volunteers, K9 handlers, and members of the public using our zone mapping system and radio protocols.

Rapid Response

We maintain a ready-response team so we can deploy quickly — often within hours of a report being received.
