OUR STORY

Born from a simple idea: no dog should be lost forever.

Noodle’s Canine Rescue was founded on the Isle of Man to provide a dedicated, professional canine search and rescue service for missing dogs. We use advanced thermal drone technology to locate lost pets in the most challenging terrain and conditions.

HOW WE HELP

Advanced Canine Search.
Compassionate Rescue.

Thermal Drone Searches

We use high-resolution thermal drones to detect missing dogs in woodland, coastal cliffs, and at night.

Ground Teams & Volunteers

Our trained volunteers and K9 handlers conduct coordinated canine search and rescue operations.

Rapid Response

We maintain a ready team so we can deploy quickly for canine search and rescue across the Isle of Man.
