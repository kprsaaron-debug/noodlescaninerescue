OUR TEAMS

Canine Search & Rescue.
Powered by Technology & Teamwork.

Drone Team

Led by Aaron O’Reilly, our Drone Team operates professional-grade thermal imaging drones capable of detecting heat signatures from significant distances, even in dense woodland, coastal areas or complete darkness. Deployed 14 times on active searches with 4 dogs located directly using thermal imaging.

Ground Search Team

Systematic physical searches on foot using proven search patterns, navigation, terrain assessment and radio discipline across coastal paths, plantations, ravines and boggy moorland. Works in close coordination with drone intelligence.

Dog Handling & Scent Dogs

Our Dog Handling team is building capability with Noodle, our 18-month-old collie currently undergoing scent discrimination training to become a fully qualified search dog for the organisation.

MILESTONE 4 June 2026

Officially Registered as Charity No. 1387

We are delighted to announce that Noodle’s Canine Rescue has been officially registered as an Isle of Man charity (No. 1387). This status strengthens our governance, fundraising capability and ability to collaborate with emergency services.
OPERATION January 2026

Successful Recovery of Toffee – 50+ Hour Search

Our teams led a complex search in Foxdale and St Marks Country Park. Working alongside landowners, police, coastguard, and dozens of public volunteers, thermal drones played a crucial role after more than 50 hours missing.
STATISTICS 2025–2026 Season

Strong Track Record of Success

55 missing dog reports handled, 100% recovery rate, 4 dogs located by thermal drone, 14 drone deployments, and 25 active searches between June 2025 and June 2026.
TRAINING Ongoing

Noodle’s Scent Dog Training Progress

Our collie Noodle continues her scent discrimination training and is making excellent progress toward becoming a fully qualified search dog for the organisation.
SUPPORT OUR OPERATIONS

Equipment & Capability Growth

Professional thermal drone now operational, supported by community fundraising, plus 4G wildlife cameras and safety equipment. In the future we look to fund a weatherproof drone to allow for more deployments.
