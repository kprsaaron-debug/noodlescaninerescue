HOW WE HELP

Canine Search & Rescue.
Using Thermal Drones.

Thermal Drone Searches

We use advanced thermal drones to locate missing dogs in woodland, coastal areas, and at night — dramatically improving recovery rates.

Ground Teams & Volunteers

Our trained volunteers and K9 handlers work together using coordinated zone mapping and radio protocols for safe, effective searches.

Rapid Response

We maintain a ready team so we can deploy quickly for canine search and rescue operations across the Isle of Man.
