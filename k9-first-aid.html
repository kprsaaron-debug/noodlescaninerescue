K9 First Aid

Essential guidance to help an injured or unwell dog while waiting for professional veterinary care.

Important:

This information is for guidance only. Always seek professional veterinary help as soon as possible. In an emergency, contact your vet or the emergency services immediately.

Common Emergency Situations

1. Breathing Difficulties

Keep the dog calm and in a comfortable position. Do not put anything in the mouth. Get to a vet immediately.

2. Bleeding

Apply firm pressure with a clean cloth. If bleeding continues, add more cloth on top (do not remove the first layer). Seek urgent veterinary care.

3. Heatstroke

Move to a cool, shaded area. Wet the dog with cool (not cold) water. Offer small amounts of water to drink. Seek emergency vet care immediately.

4. Seizures

Do not restrain the dog. Clear the area of objects. Time the seizure. Contact your vet immediately after it ends.

In an Emergency

If you find an injured dog or your own dog is in distress, contact us or your local vet immediately.
