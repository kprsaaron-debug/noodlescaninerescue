K9 First Aid Training

Professional courses delivered by qualified instructors from Noodle’s Canine Rescue IOM

Our K9 First Aid courses equip dog owners, handlers and professionals with the skills to respond confidently in emergencies. All training emphasises practical skills, animal welfare and when to seek veterinary help. Courses are suitable for the Isle of Man context (rural, coastal and working environments).

Our Training Courses

Level 2 Dog First Aid
For dog owners, walkers & handlers

Essential first aid skills for everyday situations. Ideal for pet owners, dog walkers, groomers and anyone who wants to be prepared for common canine emergencies.

What you will learn:
  • Recognising signs of emergency & primary survey (ABC)
  • Canine CPR, rescue breathing and choking (Heimlich manoeuvre)
  • Controlling bleeding, treating shock and wound care
  • Poisoning, heatstroke, hypothermia and seizures
  • Bandaging, splinting and safe transport to vet
  • When and how to contact emergency veterinary services
Duration 1 Day (8 hours)
Format Theory + Practical
Certification Level 2 Award (valid 3 years)

Level 3 Dog First Aid
For security dog handlers & professionals

Advanced training tailored for professional handlers, security dog teams, kennel staff and those working in remote or operational environments. Builds on Level 2 with trauma-focused content relevant to working dogs on the Isle of Man.

What you will learn:
  • Advanced triage and incident management
  • Trauma care: fractures, crush injuries, penetrating wounds
  • Working dog specific injuries (terrain, fatigue, environmental)
  • Remote location considerations and evacuation planning
  • Integration with emergency services and incident command
  • Legal, insurance and welfare responsibilities for professional handlers
Duration 2 Days (16 hours)
Format Theory + Extended Practical
Certification Level 3 Award (valid 3 years)
