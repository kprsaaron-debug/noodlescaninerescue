Volunteer Today

Volunteer Today

Join one of our teams and help bring missing dogs home across the Isle of Man.

Want to join one of our teams?

We are always looking for dedicated volunteers for ground searching, drone observation, logistics and community support roles. Full training is provided. Get in touch via our reporting form or email us to express interest.

Our Teams

Drone Team

Led by experienced volunteer drone pilot Aaron O’Reilly. Professional-grade thermal imaging drones for night searches, large-area coverage and real-time coordination with ground and scent dog teams.

Ground Search Team

Systematic physical searches on foot using proven search patterns, navigation and radio discipline across the Island’s coastal, plantation and moorland terrain.

Dog Handling & Scent Dogs

Working with training scent dogs including Noodle, our 18-month-old collie undergoing scent discrimination training to become a fully qualified search dog.

Training & Standards

All team members receive structured, role-specific training including search techniques, map reading and navigation (using OS maps and GPS), radio communications protocols, thermal drone operation and image interpretation, canine first aid, and safe working practices in varied Isle of Man terrain. We maintain the highest standards of safety, animal welfare and professionalism.
