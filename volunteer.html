Volunteer With Us

Help bring missing dogs home safely across the Isle of Man. No previous experience required — full training and support provided.

Why Volunteer With Noodle’s Canine Rescue?

Every year, dogs go missing on the Isle of Man — often in challenging coastal, rural or woodland terrain. Our volunteer team provides a vital, rapid response using thermal drones, systematic ground searches and trained scent dogs.

By joining us you become part of a compassionate, professional and growing community that makes a real, tangible difference to families and their beloved dogs. Whether you have a few hours a month or want to become a core team member, there is a role for you.

Ways You Can Help

Ground Search Team

Join our trained volunteers who conduct systematic foot searches in all weathers and terrains. You will learn search patterns, navigation, radio communications and safe working practices. Ideal for those who enjoy the outdoors and working as part of a coordinated team.

Full training provided • Flexible commitment

Drone Observer / Support

Assist our qualified drone pilots during operations. Roles include acting as visual observer, helping with equipment, mapping support and coordinating with ground teams. Great opportunity to learn about thermal imaging technology and search coordination.

Training provided • Good for detail-oriented volunteers

Dog Handling Support

Support our K9 handlers and help with the care, training and welfare of our scent dogs (including Noodle, our collie in training). Opportunities range from general dog handling assistance to eventual handler training for suitable volunteers.

Animal lovers especially welcome

Logistics & Operations Support

Help behind the scenes with equipment maintenance, vehicle support, mapping, communications, record keeping and organising community search days. Essential role that keeps our field teams effective and safe.

Flexible hours • Can often be done remotely

Community & Awareness

Help spread the word when a dog goes missing, manage our social media presence, organise fundraising events, or represent the charity at community events. Your voice helps more dogs get found faster.

Perfect for social media savvy volunteers

K9 First Aid & Welfare

Support our training programme or assist with canine first aid knowledge within the team. Volunteers with existing veterinary, medical or first aid experience are especially valuable, but full training is available for everyone.

Valuable skill that benefits the whole team

What We Offer Volunteers

Full Training Provided
Search techniques, radio use, navigation, thermal drone awareness, canine first aid and safe working practices — all at no cost to you.
Insurance & Support
Public liability insurance cover during official operations. We look after our volunteers with proper briefings, equipment and welfare support.
Be Part of Something Meaningful
Work alongside like-minded people who care deeply about animal welfare. Many of our volunteers say it’s one of the most rewarding things they’ve ever done.
