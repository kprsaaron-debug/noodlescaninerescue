Why we partner with Pawfit

As a canine search and rescue charity, we see first-hand how quickly a dog can go missing — and how much harder it is to find them without any form of real-time location data. GPS trackers are one of the most effective preventative tools available to owners.

Pawfit makes waterproof GPS trackers for dogs and cats that work over the mobile network (no range limit like Bluetooth tags). Features include live tracking, activity monitoring, safety zones with escape alerts, and a speaker for voice recall.

How the affiliate programme works

  • You get a discount (typically up to 10%) when you use code NOODL3 at checkout.
  • Noodle’s Canine Rescue earns a small commission on each sale (paid quarterly).
  • That commission helps fund drone batteries, 4G cameras, training and other operational costs.

There is no extra cost to you beyond the normal price of the tracker (minus the discount).
