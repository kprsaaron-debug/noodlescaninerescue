About Us

Isle of Man’s dedicated canine search and rescue charity

Overview

Noodle’s Canine Rescue IOM is the Isle of Man’s only dedicated canine search and rescue charity. We specialise in locating missing dogs using advanced thermal drone technology combined with trained ground teams and K9 handlers.

Our Aim & Purpose

Our purpose is to bring lost dogs home safely and quickly. We exist to reduce the time dogs spend missing, minimise distress to animals and families, and provide professional, compassionate support during searches.

Achievements

  • Officially registered as Charity No. 1387 in 2026
  • Successfully conducted multiple active searches using thermal drones
  • Built a trained team of volunteers across ground search, drone operations, and dog handling

Training & Standards

All team members receive structured training appropriate to their role. This includes search techniques, radio communications, map reading, thermal drone operation, and dog handling where relevant. We maintain high standards of safety, professionalism, and animal welfare.

Our Teams

Ground Search Team

Our Ground Search Team carries out physical searches on foot. They are trained in search patterns, navigation, and working safely in varied terrain including coastal and rural areas.

Drone Team

The Drone Team operates thermal drones to locate missing dogs, especially in difficult or large areas and during nighttime operations. They are trained in drone flight, thermal image interpretation, and search coordination.

Dog Handling & Scent Dogs

Our Dog Handling team works with trained scent dogs to locate missing dogs. They are experienced in handling working dogs and integrating them effectively with drone and ground search teams.
