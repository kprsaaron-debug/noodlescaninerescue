OUR STORY

About Noodle’s Canine Rescue

The Isle of Man’s dedicated search & rescue charity using thermal drone technology to reunite missing dogs with their families.

How It All Began

Noodle’s Canine Rescue was founded in response to a growing need on the Isle of Man for a dedicated, professional search and rescue service for missing dogs. What started as a passionate local initiative has grown into the Island’s only charity using advanced thermal drone technology to locate lost pets — often in the most challenging terrain and conditions.

Our Mission

We exist to bring lost dogs home safely using the best available technology and trained volunteers. Every search is carried out with care, professionalism, and an unwavering commitment to animal welfare and family reunification.

Becoming a Registered Charity

In early 2026, after months of dedicated work, we achieved official charitable status on the Isle of Man — Charity Registration No. 1387. This milestone allows us to expand our reach, train more volunteers, invest in life-saving equipment, and operate with full transparency and accountability.

Our Approach

We combine cutting-edge thermal drone technology with experienced ground teams and K9 handlers. Our operations are coordinated, professional, and always focused on the safety of both the animals and our volunteers.
